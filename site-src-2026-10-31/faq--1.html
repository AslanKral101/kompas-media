The Watch

Design, specifications and what makes a Kompas different.

What makes KOMPAS watches unique?

KOMPAS watches are inspired by the RAF Sector Clock, a historic military tool used for precision coordination. Our dials feature bold, colour coded triangular markers in five minute intervals: a functional design reborn as a modern timepiece for adventure, resilience and purpose.

Is it KOMPAS or Kompass?

It is KOMPAS, with one S. People often search for us as Kompass watch, Kompas watch or Kompas saat, and they all lead here: KOMPAS Watches, the British-designed automatic field watch brand at kompaswatches.com and @kompaswatch on Instagram. We are not connected to other companies with a similar name. The name comes from the compass: our four watches are North, East, South and West.

Are KOMPAS watches water resistant?

Yes. Every KOMPAS model is rated to 100m, built for everyday use and outdoor activities, from rain to swimming and challenging conditions.

Do KOMPAS watches have luminous features?

Yes. We use high grade Super-LumiNova® on the hands and markers for excellent readability in low light, ideal for night time use and outdoor adventures.

Are the straps interchangeable?

Yes. All KOMPAS watches use a standard 20mm lug width with quick release spring bars, so you can swap straps in seconds to match your style or activity. Every watch ships with both a rubber and a nylon strap.

Where are KOMPAS watches assembled?

KOMPAS watches are designed in Great Britain, powered by precise Japanese movements and assembled in Hong Kong by skilled watchmakers, ensuring consistent quality and reliability.

What makes KOMPAS packaging special?

Each watch comes in durable, dial inspired packaging designed for reuse. It's more than a box: it's a keepsake that reflects the brand's adventurous spirit.

Returns & Refunds

Changed your mind or received a faulty watch? Here is how it works.

Can I return or exchange my watch?

Yes. In accordance with the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations, you have the right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving your watch, for any reason. Notify us within this period, then you have a further 14 days to return the item. Exchanges are also available if you prefer a different model.

To cancel, contact us at contact@kompaswatches.com.

What condition must the watch be in?

Returned items must be:

  • Unworn and unused
  • In original condition, with all protective films intact
  • Returned in original packaging
  • Accompanied by all documentation and accessories

You may inspect the watch as you would in a shop. Handling beyond what is needed to assess it may lead to a deduction reflecting any diminished value, for example wear, strap creasing, removed films or scratches.

Who pays for return shipping?

For change of mind cancellations, you are responsible for return shipping costs. If the item is faulty or not as described, we cover reasonable return shipping costs.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds are issued within 14 days of receiving the returned goods, or of receiving proof that they have been returned. Refunds go to the original payment method.

Original standard outbound shipping is refundable where required by law. Upgraded or express shipping is non refundable.

What if my watch is faulty?

Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 are not affected. If your watch develops a fault:

  • You have a 30 day right to reject for a full refund
  • After 30 days, we may offer repair or replacement
  • If repair or replacement is unsuccessful, you may be entitled to a refund or price reduction

We may request reasonable evidence of the fault before proceeding.

2 Year Warranty

Every KOMPAS is covered by a 2 year international warranty from the date of delivery.

What does the warranty cover?

Manufacturing defects in the:

  • Movement
  • Dial
  • Hands
  • Case construction
  • Factory water resistance integrity

In the rare event of an issue, we will repair or replace your timepiece.

What is not covered?
  • Accidental, impact or drop damage
  • Cosmetic wear, including scratches and dents
  • Strap wear
  • Water damage caused by improper crown use
  • Damage caused by unauthorised repairs or modifications
  • Normal wear and tear
How do I make a claim?

Email contact@kompaswatches.com with:

  • Your order number
  • A description of the issue
  • Clear photographs
  • Proof of purchase
Who pays shipping for warranty service?

If the issue is confirmed under warranty, we cover return shipping following approval. If it is not covered, we will send a repair estimate before proceeding.

Repair timelines vary depending on parts availability and location.

05

Care & Service

A few simple habits keep your KOMPAS performing for years.

How do I take care of my KOMPAS?
  • Rinse with fresh water after saltwater exposure
  • Keep the crown fully secured before water contact
  • Clean with a soft cloth and mild soap only
  • Avoid strong impacts or dropping the watch
  • Avoid extreme heat, prolonged extreme temperatures and strong magnetic fields
  • Store in a dry place when not in use
How often should it be serviced?

Have your watch serviced every 3 to 5 years by a professional watchmaker to maintain performance.
